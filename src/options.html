Odoo Mates WhatsApp Connector

Add the address of your Odoo. Its pages can then send messages through WhatsApp Web, open in another tab of this browser.

    No Odoo address yet.

    Before sending

    1. Install the WhatsApp Connector Web module (om_whatsapp_web) in Odoo, and set the provider of your WhatsApp account to WhatsApp Web.
    2. Open web.whatsapp.com once and link it to your phone. It may then stay in a background tab.

    The extension sends only the messages you send from Odoo, one at a time. WhatsApp does not allow automated or bulk sending from WhatsApp Web.

    While WhatsApp Web is open, the extension also reads its messages for your Odoo, the WhatsApp account of which has the number linked in WhatsApp Web. Odoo keeps those of its contacts and of the chats about its documents, and the others when the account keeps unknown numbers; the rest are dropped in this browser and never sent anywhere. Groups, status and channels are not read.
